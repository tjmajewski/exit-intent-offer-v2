# Handoff — September 19, 2026

**Supersedes [HANDOFF-2026-09-18.md](./HANDOFF-2026-09-18.md)** for priority order.
That doc's traps and review history still stand; read §4 and §5 of it. Nothing
shipped today. This is the output of two full audits of the AI stack — a QA pass
and an architecture pass — read against the actual product goal:

> Give the **right discount** to the **right customer** at the **right time**,
> post add-to-cart. Learn which **message**, which **offer** and which **trigger**
> works, optimize per store, then carry meta-learnings **across stores** within
> Shopify's rules.

Every item below is graded against that sentence, not against generic code
quality. Claims marked **[verified]** were re-traced in the code before being
written here; the rest are the auditors' findings at their stated confidence.

## 0. Verdict

**What the engine actually does today:** the decision that reaches a shopper is
almost entirely hardcoded. Propensity is ~30 hand-set coefficients. Offer
magnitude is a deterministic `offerCeilingPercent` clamp. Offer shape is fixed
bands. Timing is an ordered if/else with exit-intent armed as a floor. Three
bandits are wired and receiving data (variant / show-skip / discount-vs-none);
two of the three sit below their own cold-start floors and cannot leave them at
current traffic. Roughly ten further learning constructs exist and are inert.

| Axis | Learned today? | Blocking reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Message** | Partially | 11 genes, 1 binary reward, ~2 variants per cell; serve-time brand-safety clamp can swap the headline after the impression is logged |
| **Offer** | Binary only, unreachable | `MIN_ARM_OUTCOMES = 50` × 10 propensity buckets ≈ 1,000 outcomes; magnitude has **no served-amount column** in `VariantImpression`, so it is not even analysable by hand |
| **Trigger** | No | Desktop `mouseout` is armed in all three paths, so `idle` and `exit_intent_or_idle` are byte-identical; mobile coerces `exit_intent` to a 15s timer, so the "winning" arm is the fastest timer wearing the wrong label |
| **Cross-store** | No | The aggregation cron that writes the archetype tables **has never run** |

**The single most important structural fact:** the decision is minted at
**prefetch** — `exit-intent-modal.js:2179` calls `/api/ai-decision` on every
carted page load, before any trigger. One call mints a real Shopify code, a
`DiscountOffer`, an `AIDecision`, a `VariantImpression` and an
`InterventionOutcome`. Only the *render* is conditional. Most of §1 and §2
follow from that.

## 1. Ship-blockers — money and shoppers

### 1.1 Threshold offers strand the shopper and then get credited for it
`exit-intent-modal.js:3609-3634` **[verified]**. The threshold primary CTA
navigates to the referrer / `/collections` / `/` and returns. It never builds
`/discount/<code>`, never stashes the code. The recovery pill cannot save it
either: `handleCTAClick` sets `ctaClicked = true` (:3529) and `shouldShowPill`
requires `!ctaClicked` (:3314). The shopper adds the extra items, checks out,
pays full price.

Worse for learning: `showModal` already stamped `exit_intent` on the cart
(:2542), so the order webhook credits the conversion as an intervention. **The
threshold arm is rewarded for a discount that was never delivered.** Same class
as the 9/18 fix "never strand a shopper holding a discount they earned", one
branch over.

### 1.2 A discount code per carted page load
`:1280-1293`, no session dedupe — manual mode has one at :2587, the AI path does
not. Budget is over-counted by the prefetch multiplier, because `checkBudget`
sums offers nobody ever saw. Budget exhaustion also returns *before* holdout
assignment, so control-group members get a modal and no holdout row.

### 1.3 Pro shops have no promo-stacking guard
`signals.hasPromoActive` is read in two places and **written nowhere**
**[verified]** — only `baseline-selector.js:127,143` and
`ai-decision.jsx:750` consume it. The real guard is Enterprise-gated
(`ai-decision.jsx:441`) and the codes are issued `combinesWith: all`
(`discount-codes.js:481`). A Pro merchant running 20% site-wide has Resparq
stack 15% on top of it. This is a refund conversation with the first paying
merchant, and it also makes `chose not to discount` partly unreachable — which
means **the 9/18 open question has four candidates, not two**.

### 1.4 Two smaller engine faults
- Unique-code branch leaves `discountResult` undefined on an unrecognised
  `decision.type` → 500 → no modal and no suppression row (`:1281-1299`).
- Generic-code drift sync overwrites `decision.amount` **after** the margin
  guard has run (`:1224-1233`).

## 2. The measurement is lying in both directions

Do not read a lift number, or raise the holdout, until this section is closed.

1. **Holdout CVR is deflated → reported lift inflated.** The order webhook
   *creates* a second holdout row on conversion
   (`webhooks.orders.create.jsx:289` → `recordInterventionOutcome`, which is a
   plain `db.interventionOutcome.create` at
   `intervention-threshold.server.js:232`) **[verified]** instead of updating the
   existing one. `getIncrementality` then computes `c/(n+c)`. This is the exact
   number behind the "holdout measures real lift per store" claim. The same
   double-write hits the skip arm.
2. **ITT lift is deflated by the fire-rate shortfall.** Holdout and skip
   attributes are stamped on the cart at *decision* time (`:2733`, `:2752`); the
   shown attribute is stamped at *render* time (`:2545`). A decided-but-never-
   rendered session stamps nothing, so its conversions can never be attributed —
   yet the row sits in the ITT denominator. Moving the stamp to decision time is
   a moved line and is the highest value-per-hour item in this document.
3. **Show and skip/holdout sample different populations.** Skip and holdout are
   minted at prefetch for every carted visitor; show only counts confirmed
   renders, i.e. visitors who triggered. Non-triggering visitors convert more.
   This biases `shouldIntervene` toward skip and makes the incrementality
   comparison apples-to-oranges regardless of (1) and (2).
4. `rebuildDiscountArmStats` lacks `rendered: true` as well as the time filter
   9/18 already tracked. `evolution-cycle.js:61` triggers generations on
   unrendered impressions.
5. **`confirm-render` is fire-and-forget and is the sole gate on all show-side
   learning.** If it is blocked — ad blocker, CSP, flaky network — the engine
   learns "never show" with zero signal that anything is wrong. Nothing computes
   a confirmed-render rate. One metric would catch this whole class.

## 3. Machinery that is believed to work and does not

- **The cross-store aggregation cron has never run** **[verified]**.
  `apps.exit-intent.api.aggregate-meta-learning.jsx` has no caller: absent from
  `scripts/ops/cron-machines.sh` `JOBS`, absent from `package.json`, referenced
  nowhere in code — and it calls `authenticate.admin()`, so a Fly machine could
  not call it anyway. `archetype_performance_by_key` / `_by_vertical` have never
  existed. Archetype priors levels 2 and 3 are therefore **dead, not cold**.
- **The archetype multiplier is a mathematical no-op** **[verified]**.
  `variant-engine.js:747-750` keys the multiplier on `variant.baseline`, but
  every contender comes from `getLiveVariants(shopId, baseline, segment)` — one
  baseline, one archetype, one constant. Scaling every sample by the same
  constant cannot change the argmax. It logs "Archetype biasing active" while
  doing nothing.
- **Hybrid shops get no crons at all** **[verified]**. `app.settings.jsx:527`
  writes `mode: 'hybrid'`; all four crons filter `mode: 'ai'`
  (`evolution-cycle.js:16`, `threshold-learning-cycle.js:17`,
  `aggregate-gene-performance.js:33`, `track-seasonal-patterns.js:10`).
  Meanwhile the decision endpoint runs the full engine for them. Population
  frozen at generation 0 forever, thresholds never recalculated, no clustering.
  Entirely silent.
- **`recalculateThresholds` writes `shouldShow` / `confidence` that nothing
  anywhere reads.**
- `app/cron/track-seasonal-patterns.js` is a write-only loop — no readers outside
  the GDPR deletion paths.
- Dead: `aggregateStarterLearnings`, `getBestStarterSettings`,
  `signal_correlation`, `copy_pattern`; `getMetaInsight` /
  `shouldUseMetaLearning` are dead imports at `ai-decision.jsx:5`.
- Flag-gated off by default: surface arm, template priors, propensity model.
- Cluster priors — the one statistically sound mechanism (`blendWithPrior`) —
  return `null` below 2-3 stores, and `aggregate-gene-performance.js:54`
  early-returns entirely below 3.
- `isPreview` is client-supplied (`:78`) and disables all learning writes. It is
  the one signal in that file not hardened against forgery.
- Segment space has collapsed: the storefront sends `accountStatus:
  'logged_in'` (`:909`) and `segment-key.js` allows only
  `guest|returning|loyal` **[verified]**, so every logged-in shopper keys to
  `a:unknown`.
- Gene inheritance applies one `distinct: ['geneType']` gene set to all
  `provenTarget` variants → five byte-identical arms out of ten on Enterprise.

## 4. Making the three axes learnable at one store

The plan doc reasons everywhere about whether arms will converge while treating
the **number of cells as fixed**. That is the wrong constant to hold.

**Collapse the cell space first.** One segment until 500 impressions; three
propensity buckets, not ten; three baselines, not five. ~2 days, no new math,
roughly 10× the effective per-cell sample, and it changes the arithmetic of
every other item in this document and in `LEARNING_EXPANSION_PLAN.md`.

Then, per axis:

- **Trigger.** Make the arms distinct before anything else: honour the trigger
  gene and add `eligibleTriggers(device)` so mobile stops relabelling a 15s
  idle timer as exit intent. `Variant.impressions` must also count a trigger
  that never fired, or a non-firing arm is never punished. Only *after* this
  does the render-rate-by-device query in plan §7 mean anything — it settles
  contamination-vs-reach, and today it would answer the wrong question.
- **Offer.** Add `offeredAmount` / `offeredType` / `armSet` to
  `VariantImpression` now (half a day) so magnitude becomes analysable, even by
  hand, while the bandit is still years of traffic away. Magnitude as a learned
  arm later: 3-arm perturbation, globally pooled — not per shop.
- **Message.** Log the copy that was actually rendered, after the brand-safety
  clamp. Do not expand the arm space (generated copy is the wrong direction
  right now); score the signals already being collected instead —
  `modalShowCount`, `ignoreStreak`, `daysSinceLastShow`.

Fixed-arm epochs (freeze the other axes while one is under test) are the cheapest
route to interpretable results with several learners choosing simultaneously.

## 5. Cross-store and Shopify compliance — submission risk

No PII, email, order id, product name or shop domain crosses a store boundary.
That part is sound. **The gates are the problem**, and two of them arm
themselves automatically at install #3.

1. **`aggregate-gene-performance.js:120` is `&&` where it must be `||`**
   **[verified]**: `storeCount < minStores && agg.totalImpressions < 100`. One
   store with 100+ impressions on a gene publishes its **copy strings** and
   **absolute revenue dollars** into the global pool at `sampleSize: 1`. Latent
   only because the cron needs three shops to run at all. One character.
2. `template-priors.js:88` reads `MetaLearningGene` with **no sampleSize gate**
   (the other reader requires ≥3), so those k=1 rows reach another merchant's
   runtime sampling.
3. `MIN_PRIOR_STORES = 2` is below any k-anonymity bar — with two stores in a
   vertical × AOV band, either can subtract to recover the other's numbers.
4. `contributeToMetaLearning` defaults **true** and is editable only in the
   **super-admin** console. No merchant-facing disclosure or control exists.
5. **No Shopify Customer Privacy API integration at all** — `customerPrivacy` /
   `trackingConsent` appear nowhere — while the extension writes a persistent
   `localStorage` `resparqVisitorId` and a 30-day behavioural log. This is the
   largest app-review risk in the stack.
6. `webhooks.shop.redact.jsx` misses `VisitorTouch` (durable `visitorId`),
   `VariantSegmentStat`, `EvolutionCursor`, `AdminAuditLog` and the
   `${shopId}::…` `MetaLearningInsights` rows. None has an FK, so `shop.delete`
   succeeds and the webhook reports clean success — the same failure shape its
   own header describes.

**Envelope to design to.** May cross a boundary: aggregate ratios over k≥5
stores with bounded per-store weight; Resparq's own taxonomy (archetypes,
templateIds, triggerTypes); Resparq-authored copy; coarse cluster dimensions as
keys. Must not: merchant-authored copy, discount codes, product names, absolute
per-store revenue / AOV / order counts, anything visitor-level.

Statistically, `MetaLearningGene` pooling is naive `Σconv/Σimp` with no store
random effect (Simpson's-paradox-prone), and the archetype/template
rank→multiplier steps are not shrinkage estimators. Target: a single
`posteriorFor()` doing empirical-Bayes global→cluster→shop→cell, with the
rank-multipliers deleted.

## 9. Traps — additions to 9/18 §4

- **The decision is minted at prefetch, not at show.** Any sentence of the form
  "the AI decided X" is about a page load, not about a shopper who saw anything.
- **A learner existing, being wired, and being able to move are three different
  things.** Three of thirteen are wired; two of those three cannot leave cold
  start at this traffic.
- **A multiplier applied uniformly to every contender is not a bias, it is a
  no-op.** Check the variation across contenders, not the presence of the term.
- **Two of the compliance gates arm themselves on install #3**, not on deploy.
  They will pass every test you run today.
- **`mode: 'hybrid'` is invisible to every cron.** Any statement about what the
  learning system does is false for hybrid shops.
